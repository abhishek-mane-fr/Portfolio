01 / Overview

From bundle setup to storefront offer.

BundleForge is a personal app project focused on helping merchants configure bundle offers and related upsell experiences. The interface and supporting data models are being developed as part of the project; this page documents the current direction rather than presenting a released app.

01 / BUNDLES

Bundle creation flow

A step-based configuration experience for defining bundle details, selecting products, setting offer rules and reviewing the setup.

02 / OFFER LOGIC

Discount configuration

Exploring bundle discount options and Shopify Functions as part of the app’s discount implementation.

03 / STOREFRONT

Storefront widget

Work toward presenting bundle offers within a merchant’s storefront so customers can discover the offer while shopping.

04 / UPSELLS

Upsell workflows

Separate screens and data models for creating and managing upsell offers as a related part of the app experience.
